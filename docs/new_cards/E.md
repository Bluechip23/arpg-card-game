# New sheet cards — batch E (big spells, zones, summon cards)

What the code does for each card, in the style of `docs/CARD_REFERENCE.md`.
All eight live in `scripts/cards/new_cards_e.gd` (`NewCardsE`); the shared
files only carry one-line hooks into it. Tests: `tests/test_new_cards_e.gd`.

Hooks: `Card._execute_card` (the Ward's armor), `DeckManager.greedy_tax`
(tempo cuts shown on the hand and paid by the play),
`BuffManager.sync_flag_buffs` (badges), `Summon.take_damage` /
`get_status_effects` (Brace), `Enemy._deal_damage_to_player` (the lion),
`SummonedSkeleton` (the `pride_lion` kind), and in `main.gd`:
`play_selected_card`, `_resolve_queued_card` (before / after the card),
`_apply_card_world_effects_inner`, the tempo tick, `_on_enemy_movement_completed`,
`_on_all_enemies_defeated`, and `show_full_card_picker` (a negative
`pick_count` now means "any number, up to that many").

A hero's standing effects (the Ward's window, the Ledger and its bank, the
Candle's charges, the Pride mark and lion) are metadata on their
`PlayerStats`; fog zones and crates are metadata on `Main`.

---

- **Ward of Hallow Glass** (`ward_of_hallow_glass`) — Legendary · 70 mana, 6 tempo (resolves tick 2) · targets self · slots: bulwark, buckler, wand, gem · Defense, Spell
  - Text: Gain armor equal to 6 plus half your INT. For 10 tempo, while you have armor, your offensive spells cost 1 less tempo.
  - Code: the card's block is raised by `floor(INT / 2)` for the play and granted through `Card._execute_block` → `PlayerStats.add_armor`, the path every Defense card uses (on-self block, Thick Steel / Earth Book, flat block bonuses, Empower's cut). A 10-tempo window then starts (a recast restarts it at 10). While it runs and `get_total_armor() > 0`, every card in hand with `school == SPELL` and `is_offensive()` costs 1 less tempo (never below 0): the cut rides `DeckManager.greedy_tax`, so the card face shows it and the play pays it. A "Ward of Hallow Glass" badge counts the window down.
  - Mismatch / open question: the armor does not draw on Bolster — no card's `add_armor` does today (`add_armor_with_bolster` is used by one executor only); it follows the common path. · The window's clock starts when the card resolves (tick 2), so its own last 4 ticks run against it. · The cut reaches hand plays; Quiver / Manifest plays do not read `greedy_tax`.

- **The Ledger** (`the_ledger`) — Mythic · 130 mana, 5 tempo (resolves tick 5) · targets self · slots: wand, staff, crown, gem · Utility, Spell
  - Text: For the rest of the fight, every direct hit you land with an offensive card banks 10% of its damage. Playing a Defense card spends the bank as healing.
  - Code: sets a standing effect on the caster. Around every hand-played card's resolution (`_resolve_queued_card`: executor plus world effects) each living enemy's health + armor is read before and after; if the card `is_offensive()`, 10% of the total actually removed is added to the bank (a float, so small hits add up). When a card of the Defense type or with the `defense` tag resolves, the caster heals for `floor(bank)` (through `PlayerStats.heal`, so healing bonuses apply) and the bank is emptied. A "The Ledger" badge carries the banked number. It ends when all enemies are defeated (`_on_all_enemies_defeated`).
  - Mismatch / open question: **the sheet gives no duration and no Maintain** — it is coded as "the rest of the fight", for free after the 130 mana. Should it be a Power with an upkeep, or timed? · "Direct hit" = damage dealt while the offensive card itself resolves; DoT ticks, zones, thorns, summons, auto attacks and Quiver / Manifest plays do not bank. · Overkill does not bank (only health and armor actually removed). · Defensive instants (Vengeful Shield) fire from the hand, are not "played", and do not spend the bank. · A recast keeps the existing bank.

- **Both Sides of the Candle** (`both_sides_of_the_candle`) — Mythic · 150 mana, 0 tempo · range 6 · targets self / ally · slots: dagger, spear, crown · Utility
  - Text: The target's next 5 attack cards cost 2 less tempo. Each applies 8 Burn to the enemies it hits and deals 10% of the attacker's own max health to themselves, past armor.
  - Code: puts 5 charges on the targeted hero (yourself when no ally is clicked); a recast resets to 5. While charges remain, that hero's `is_attack()` cards cost 2 less tempo (never below 0; shown on the hand, via `greedy_tax`). A charge is spent as the attack is **played** (as Tighten String's is), and that play is marked: when it resolves, every enemy it damaged gets `apply_debuff("burn", 8)` and the attacker takes `max(1, floor(max_health * 0.10))` through `take_direct_damage` (armor and temp HP do not absorb it, nothing reduces it) — hit or miss. A charge badge shows the count.
  - Mismatch / open question: "attacks" = Attack cards (`is_attack()`): the auto attack, offensive spells and Quiver / Manifest plays neither spend a charge nor burn. · The self-damage can kill (it is the game's self-paid-cost path). · An attack whose target dies before it resolves returns to hand with its charge already spent (Tighten String behaves the same). · On an NPC ally (the dojo's practice allies) the charges sit unused — they play no cards.

- **Thickened Nature** (`thickened_nature`) — Rare · 120 mana, 5 tempo (resolves tick 5) · range 8 · targets point · slots: wand, staff, buckler, crown · Utility
  - Text: Create a dense fog 3 squares across for 15 tempo. Enemies inside are held Slowed 1, and one that ends a move inside is Rooted 5.
  - Code: a ground zone of the aimed tile and the 8 around it, 15 tempo. Each tempo, any living enemy standing in it with no Slow stack gets `apply_debuff("slow", 1)` — held at one stack, not piled up (a stack is 5 tempo, so it is re-applied as it runs out and lingers up to 5 tempo after leaving). `Enemy.movement_completed` on a fog tile → `apply_debuff("root", 5)` (5 tempo; Root timers add). Several fogs may stand at once. Fogs lift when all enemies are defeated.
  - Mismatch / open question: **art** — the packs hold no fog or cloud tile (`tile_fog.png` is the fog-of-war's dark slab). The fog reuses the Territorial Mark's ground-smoke disc (`main._make_mark_smoke_visual`), paled to grey. A drawn fog is on the art list. · "Rooted 5" is read as 5 tempo. · An enemy pushed or pulled into the fog is not "ending a move" and is only Slowed. · Enemies that are already Slowed from elsewhere get no extra stack.

- **Flames of Alexandria** (`flames_of_alexandria`) — Legendary · 200 mana, 15 tempo (resolves tick 10) · cone 3 · targets self · slots: staff · Utility, Spell
  - Text: Discard your hand, shuffle your discard pile into your draw pile, then draw 10 (up to your hand size). Discard any number of the cards drawn: for each, deal 3 fire damage in a cone in front of you and apply 1 Burn to yourself.
  - Code: on resolve — (1) every card in hand is discarded through `discard_card_from_hand` (true discards: `card_discarded` / `non_play_discard` fire); (2) the discard pile, minus this card, is shuffled into the draw pile; (3) `draw_card()` is called 10 times; (4) the picker (`show_full_card_picker`, any-number mode) offers the cards drawn; Confirm with none, or Cancel, discards nothing. (5) For each chosen card: it is discarded, every enemy in the cone takes one hit of `get_effective_spell_damage(3)` fire damage, and the caster gains 1 Burn. The cone is 3 squares long, 60° wide (the AoE indicator's cone), facing the cursor as the spell resolves (the caster's heading if the cursor is on their tile); the searing-fire effect plays along it.
  - Mismatch / open question: **hand limit** — the draws are ordinary draws, exactly as Balance of Alnilam's 10 and Meditate's: the hand cap holds (Brad's is 4) and the draws past it are overdraws (overflow effects / Overdraw riders fire, the cards stay on the pile). So "draw 10" is usually "draw to a full hand" and the damage tops out at hand size × 3. If the sheet means 10 cards in hand regardless of the cap, that is a rule change for the designer. · **Tagged Spell only**, so it is a utility spell: not `is_offensive()` — no Attune, Ward discount, Ledger bank, on-hit riders, crit or Strengthen — though it reads like an offensive one. · "For each card discarded" is read as covering both the 3 damage and the 1 self-Burn, and as separate hits (INT is added to each 3). · The hand discarded in step 1 does not count toward the damage. · The spell cannot draw itself.

- **Surprise Pick Up** (`surprise_pick_up`) — Rare · 250 mana, 6 tempo (resolves tick 6) · range 5 · targets point · slots: wand, bulwark · Utility · Jail 40
  - Text: A crate lands on the tile. The first ally to stand on it for 5 tempo chooses: gain 20 temp HP, mana, armor, Thorns and Keen 20% (10 tempo) — or draw 3 cards, gain Blessed 2 for 3 cycles and restore all their Brain and Flash points. Jail 40.
  - Code: a crate stays on the aimed tile until claimed (it outlasts the fight). Each tempo, a hero or NPC ally (`main._all_allies()`; never a summon) standing on it banks tempo; stepping off resets their count. At 5 the crate opens: a player-controlled hero gets the game's choice prompt (`_show_choice_picker`) between the two bundles — closing the prompt without choosing leaves the crate and restarts the count; an NPC ally takes one at random. Bundle 1: `add_temp_health(20, 15)`, `gain_mana(20)`, `add_armor(20)`, Thorns 20 (15 tempo), Keen 20% (10 tempo). Bundle 2: draw 3 (hand cap applies), `Buff.create_blessed(2, 3)`, Brain and Flash points refilled.
  - Mismatch / open question: **the sheet says "or" and names no chooser** — coded as the opener's choice (50/50 for NPC allies). · The sheet gives no durations: temp HP 15 tempo, Thorns 15 tempo (the buff's default), Keen 10 tempo are guesses. · "20 mana" is 20 real mana units (not temp mana), tiny beside the 250 cost — is 200 meant? · **Art**: the packs have no crate (`docs/ASSET_PACKS.md`: "no crates or barrels"; `goods_crate` is our own drawing), so it wears the treasure pack's closed wooden chest (`CraftpixProps` `chest_wood_closed`). · Enemies can stand on the tile; nothing happens.

- **Inspiring Whistle** (`inspiring_whistle`) — Rare · 100 mana, 5 tempo (resolves tick 3) · range anywhere (line of sight) · targets enemy / self · slots: wand, sword, axe · Utility
  - Text: Choose an enemy (or click elsewhere for the nearest). Your summons move up to 3 tiles toward it and gain Brace 30% for 2 hits, doubled for a summon with it in reach. With no summons, draw a card instead.
  - Code: `.tres` changed to `target_types = ["enemy", "self"]`, ranged with infinite range, so the enemy clicked is the one chosen; a click on anything else (or an enemy that died during the wind-up) picks the living enemy nearest the caster. Every summon of the caster (`main._all_summons()` whose `summoner` is the caster or unset) steps up to 3 tiles toward it (4-way greedy steps through `_summon_can_step`, stopping beside it; a Stunned / Rooted / Frozen summon stays), then gets `brace_percent = 30`, `brace_hits = 2` — 60% if the enemy is then within the summon's own reach (adjacent for the biters; 6 squares for a spirit bow). `Summon.take_damage` cuts the next 2 non-status hits by that share. The Allies tab shows a Brace badge. No summons: draw a card.
  - Mismatch / open question: the sheet says "Utility, self" with no targeting; choosing an enemy needed a target, so the card now takes an enemy at any range in line of sight. · Brace on a summon is a counter on the `Summon` class (summons have no `BuffManager`); a re-cast replaces it. · "Within range" is read as the summon's own attack reach after the move. · A summon with no enemy to go to still gets Brace 30%.

- **Stoked Pride** (`stoked_pride`) — Rare · 100 mana, 5 tempo (resolves tick 4) · range 6 · targets ally (yourself when no ally is clicked) · slots: none · Utility
  - Text: Mark an ally. When an enemy next attacks them, a lion (30 health) is summoned beside them and takes the damage instead. While it lives within 2 squares of them, it keeps taking the attacks aimed at them.
  - Code: marks the hero (a badge; the mark waits until it triggers, with no clock). In `Enemy._deal_damage_to_player`, when an enemy attack is about to damage a marked hero, a lion is raised on the nearest free tile to them and the attack's damage lands on the lion; the hero takes none and the hit's riders do not fire. The lion is a `SummonedSkeleton` of kind `pride_lion`: 30 health, no attack, a summon of the caster (healable, targetable by enemies, outside the 3-summon cap, moved by Inspiring Whistle, lives until killed). It does not march with the summoner; each tempo it steps one tile back toward its ward when more than a square away. While it lives and stands within 2 squares of the ward it takes every such enemy attack on them in full (overkill is lost with it).
  - Mismatch / open question: **the sheet gives the lion no stats** — 30 health, no attack, and the "keeps intercepting while within 2 squares" guard are all guesses. Should it fight? Should it leave after the one attack? Should it have a lifetime? · **Art**: the packs hold no lion (the White Manticore is a winged lion with a scorpion tail); the lion wears the Sabertooth battler, the packs' big cat. · "Attacked" = enemy attacks through `_deal_damage_to_player`; DoT ticks, ground hazards and self-damage pass the lion by. · A summon cannot be marked (the mark lives on a hero's stats). · If no tile within 3 of the ally is free, the mark waits for a later attack. · The mark and the lion persist between fights (one continuous journey).

## Designer rulings, 2026-11-12 (these replace the entries above where they differ)

- Flames of Alexandria is an **offensive spell**; its per-card damage is read while the play is open, so Attune counts. It **draws 10 and overdraws** (designer, second ruling the same day): with a hand size under 10 the draws past a full hand are overdraws; a hand size of 10 or more takes all 10.
- The Ledger is a **Maintain** (130 upkeep): it stands while maintained, and dropping it loses the bank. It no longer ends when the enemies fall.
