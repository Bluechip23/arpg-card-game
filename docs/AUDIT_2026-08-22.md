# Items & Cards Audit — 2026-08-22

Full re-audit of items and cards (all six domains swept in parallel:
weapons/shields, gauntlets/belts/boots, helms/chests/rings/mythics, attack
cards, defense/utility/power/reaction cards, and the cross-cutting plumbing —
slotting, granted cards, drop tables, save/load). Every finding below was
hand-verified in code before fixing. Prior audits: `AUDIT_FINDINGS.md`
(2026-08-07), `CODE_AUDIT.md` (2026-07-23) — no regressions of their fixes
were found.

## Fixed — world transitions / save-load (the deep cluster)

The deck snapshot used to serialize card **ids only**, and equipment stats
were restored by bare array assignment. Crossing any zone/town boundary
therefore: severed every item↔card link (granted cards duplicated on
re-equip and leaked into the deck forever; slotted cards lost their slot
bonuses), zeroed ~35 equipment channel fields (crit, lifesteal, resistances,
flat DR, …), reset jail timers / burden plays / erase timers / enhancement,
and made town card purchases and cullings silently vanish.

Now:
- `save_deck_state` carries the **live Card instances** (Resources survive
  scene changes) plus disk-safe per-card state dicts (id, jail, burden,
  erase, enhanced). `ProgressionIO.to_disk` strips the live refs.
- `restore_deck_state` prefers live instances (links intact), falls back to
  state dicts for disk loads; old id-only saves still restore.
- All equipment channel fields round-trip via `EQUIPMENT_SAVE_FIELDS`
  (data-driven list in `player_stats.gd`, so it can't silently drift again).
- `apply_equipped_item_card_effects()` runs after inventory restore (also
  fixes items equipped in town, where no deck manager exists).
- Town buys/culls now edit the carried deck snapshot directly.

## Fixed — wrong behavior

- Consecutive Snap's sticky redraw reset restored the pre-rescale 3 mana
  (now 30).
- Healthy Bliss: two duplicate implementations; the timer never reset, so
  the card went permanently inert after one trigger. Single stat-scaled,
  co-op-aware implementation now; `cycles_in_hand` resets on draw.
- Try This! backfire was permanent; now the reverse also reverts after 10
  tempo (tracking the actual mana cut so the floor never over-refunds).
- Hallowed Trunk's armor-loss accumulator was corrupted by Briarhide/
  Adimantium break reactions (reaction armor arrived before the loss was
  measured). Loss is now banked before `armor_broken` fires.
- Premeditated ignored Vulnerable/Marked/Expose (missing `from_player`).
- Energy Ball / Magic Barrier / Mana Surge carried a 1-tempo in-hand erase
  fuse: Energy Ball deleted itself before it could be played (and a played
  one recycled from discard); Magic Barrier evaporated before its trigger.
  All three are `erase_on_play` now.
- Deadeye Form and Caster Boots permanently mutated `bonus_damage` each
  play (compounding). Both tracked and reverted per play now.
- The on-self Cold rider hardcoded 1 stack on the debuff-manager path.
- Sabre Tooth's extra attack-speed tick also advanced the "real attack"
  streaks (Wizard Hat, sneakers, free-hand echo). Now counter-only.
- Oops / If Pigs Could Fly / Trip skipped Strengthen and crit (face showed
  them); they and What's the Worst? also under-reported `last_damage_dealt`
  (lifesteal/logs).
- Shields took the 10% off-hand stat penalty despite the code's own
  exemption rule; DET dodged the off-hand scaling entirely. Both fixed
  symmetrically (equip + unequip).
- Colored slots (Mauls Sabre, Feral Evocation) were positional: unslotting
  one card silently reassigned the survivor's color/payload/combo. Cards now
  stamp their slot index for their whole stay; freed colors can be refilled.
- A card could theoretically be slotted into two items (UI-only guard);
  `can_slot_card` now rejects already-slotted cards.
- Hannibals Mask granted its 15% lifesteal on EVERY attack; now on-self
  (slotted cards) only, per its text.
- Band of Aid (and all on-self heal/block riders) did nothing on cards
  without their own heal/block; they now land directly as heal/armor.
- Mend granted armor from max-health % even at full health; now armor =
  health actually restored.
- Phoenix Feather's burn backlash fired once per burn *source*; now once
  per play, per its text.
- Feathered Hat carried overflow across crits; now resets to 0, per text.
- Vengeful Shield stunned within 2.0 tiles ("melee range" = 1.5).
- Cinquedea spent its mana and consumed the manifest blade before the
  range-4 check; a whiff now costs nothing.

## Fixed — gauntlet skills (targeting rework)

- Targeted skills used `enemies[0]` — an arbitrary enemy anywhere on the
  map — and Suck fired at the UI button's world position. Targeted skills
  now **arm like a card**: click the enemy (or point, for Suck) to fire.
  Melee skills (Slice, Lethal Poke, legacy strikes) require an adjacent
  target; Coming in! validates its 5-square range and a free landing cell.
- Cooldown/mana/tempo were consumed even when a skill no-opped; validation
  now happens before any cost is paid.
- Slice charged 3 tempo total (2 + the generic 1); now 2 as described.
- House Rule now opens a **picker over the discard pile** (cost paid only
  on an actual pick) instead of grabbing the top discard.

## Fixed — shop/economy/data

- The Card Dealer sold ~55 item-kit and token cards (Ragnarok, smoke bomb,
  splinter, …); the shop now excludes everything in `DROP_EXCLUDED_CARD_IDS`.
- Vendor sell lists showed every weapon twice (QUIVER shares the weapon
  array) and listed swords under the accessory vendor; rows now filter by
  actual item type.
- Selling an item silently destroyed its enchanted cards; sale is refused
  while cards are slotted (same invariant as the Item Forge).
- Return Scroll and the tutorial Wooden Sword rolled as random loot;
  excluded from the drop pools (Bladed Doughnut stays — Mythic Mold
  redemption enumerates through the same list).
- A chest card slotted straight into an item never entered the deck; it now
  joins the draw pile as every enchanted card should.
- Lv3 tooltips understated stats by 1 wherever the automatic Lv2 "+1 to
  every positive flat bonus" persisted (18 descriptions corrected across
  mythic weapons, helms, chests, rings, gauntlets).
- Enlightened was reworked to a flat +10% crit (Megingjord pass); Lady Luck
  and Tighten String texts still promised +30%/+20% — texts aligned to +10%.
- Stale texts/prints: Territorial Mark now states its 35-health cost; Sky
  Fall print said "2 turns" (10 tempo); Mark comment said cycles (raw
  tempo); Boots of Speed comment said 51 (36); burden print showed the
  pre-rescale mana bump.

## Fixed — stale tests

- `test_attack_speed` asserted a removed sqrt-encumbrance model and read a
  deleted constant, aborting mid-run (looked like a hang). Rewritten to the
  README's current model (0..+7 ratio penalty, +10 overburdened, flat
  25-tempo draw timer).
- `test_conversion_keystones` (Arcane Blood) and `test_audit_batch2`
  (Burden) used pre-mana-rescale numbers.

## Known, deliberately not changed (design questions)

- **Legendary Lv3s are empty** for every legendary without
  `level_3_overrides` (7 gauntlets, 7 boots, 7 belts + others): the forge
  consumes 4 copies for zero effect. Needs a design pass (give each a Lv3,
  or cap legendaries at Lv2).
- **Cursed damage reduction** is baked into card-face numbers but ~15
  execute paths never apply it (they deal MORE than shown while Cursed).
  Needs a consistency pass through the per-card helpers.
- **Release Tension** always removes exactly 1 stack (heal 3); the "×3 per
  debuff removed" scaling clause can never exceed 3.
- **Repelled Block**'s fully-blocked branch consumes no armor.
- **Bouncing Shield** permanently loses half your armor ("while it is in
  the air" reads as temporary) and caps the chain at 5 targets.
- **Cryonics** "cannot act" is only enforced in multiplayer.
- **Captain Planets Circlet** doesn't count Strengthen/Regen applied to
  allies (only the player's own buff manager is relayed).
- **Ring trigger/effect enum system** is dormant (no factory uses it) and
  partially unimplemented; a future ring using it would silently no-op.
- **Lead Arrow** "Requires high ground" is never enforced (the 1.8x works
  from flat ground).
- **Vendor economy is currencyless**: buying and selling move no gold.
- **Wrist Rocket** says "permanently"; the crit bank now survives world
  transitions (saved), but not across full sessions (equipment is not
  disk-serialized).
