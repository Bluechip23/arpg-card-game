# Outstanding content — what still needs work

Every enemy, boss, character, summon and NPC, grouped by entity, with what is missing and a rough size: **S** is a few hours (a board, a number, a spawn line), **M** a day or two (several boards, an AI pass), **L** a design-plus-build job (a whole kit).

The facts come from the code and the art folders, regenerated by `python3 tools/audit/needs_update.py`:
- the sheets each figure has on disk;
- each non-movement action and whether a board plays for it;
- `TODO(sheet)` and "first pass" markers in the action's code;
- every spawn source;
- the open sheet values in `docs/ENEMY_AUDIT.md`.

The sizes are my estimate.

**Applies to everything: sounds.** The project has no audio at all: no `.ogg`, `.wav` or `.mp3` files and no `AudioStreamPlayer`. Every entity below lists "sounds: none". Building the audio pipeline (bus, player pool, a hook on `action_fired` and card play) is an **M** before any per-entity sound is an **S**.

**Not listed as gaps:**
- Movement animations for enemies: designer, 2026-11-04 — enemies show their idle pose and step.
- Figures drawn from purchased packs (Craftpix, MonsterKit): per CLAUDE.md, the packs *are* the game's art.
- Stances: no enemy, character or summon has stances in the code or on the design sheet. Nothing is missing, but if stances are intended they are an **L** design item.
