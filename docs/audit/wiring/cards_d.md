# Wiring audit: cards, slice D

51 cards, Poisoned Blood to Skeleton Army. Each card's `description` (its `.tres`) was checked against what actually runs: `Card._execute_card` in `scripts/cards/card.gd`, the card's branch in `Main._apply_card_world_effects`, the instant trigger sites in `scripts/core/main.gd` (`_fire_instant_site_effect`, `_on_enemy_debuff_applied`, `_on_enemy_damaged`, `_on_player_damage_taken`, `_on_ally_damage_taken`, `_on_attack_fully_blocked`, `_try_call_penguin`), `DeckManager.play_card` / `execute_deferred_card`, and the buff, debuff and stats code each card leans on.

**The test.** All checks are in `tests/test_wiring_cards_d.gd` (about 210 checks, under a minute). It boots `main.tscn` as the dojo (Brad), clears the dojo's chickens, and works on an open patch of floor. Enemies are training dummies (they never act), except where the card needs a real species (Polymorph's goblins, Repelled Block's melee goblin, Release Soul's Spirit Collector). Allies are the dojo's ally dummies, which are real `Player` nodes. The innate 5% crit is cancelled during the run with a -5 Keen, so damage numbers are exact.

**Costs.** Every playable card is played through the real input path: `Main.select_card`, then `play_selected_card`, then the tick clock is drained one tick at a time. Each card charges exactly the `mana_cost` and `tempo_cost` in its `.tres`. Six cards leave `tempo_cost` out and use the default of 4: Premeditated, Reckless Strike, Rise, Serene Center, Shadows and Shield Ready. Provider's Burden adds +10 mana and +1 tempo per earlier play. The instants are refused from the hand and are fired from their real trigger sites.

**What a check sees.** The test records the game state at the moment the card resolves (through `TempoManager.card_resolved`), because armor decay, mana regen and debuff clocks keep running for the rest of the card's wind-up. A status that lands on tick 1 also loses that tick, so "for N tempo" means N ticks counting the tick it lands on. This is how the engine counts every timed status, not just these cards.

**Heals and damage.** Heal numbers run through `PlayerStats.heal`, which adds the INT and healing-gear bonus. Damage numbers are scaled by STR. This applies to every card in the game, so it is not counted as a mismatch here.

Every playable card also has a `<name> charges M mana / T tempo through the play path` check in the same file.

**Shared fix: point cards land where they were aimed.** A point-targeted card (`"point"` in `target_types`) recorded its tile at the click (`_last_aim_world`, stored in the queue entry as `aim_world`). `_apply_card_world_effects` ignored it and read the live cursor when the card resolved, after its wind-up. Any point card with tempo > 0 therefore landed wherever the mouse had wandered to. The sim worked around this by re-parking the mouse before each resolve (`sim_runner._park_mouse_for_next_resolve`). Now `_resolve_queued_card` carries the stored aim in `_resolving_aim` for the card's world effects, and `_apply_card_world_effects` uses it in place of the cursor. This is `scripts/core/main.gd`, shared with every other slice's point cards (Blink, Fireball, Heroic Leap, the summon cards…). It only changes where a point card lands, and only when the cursor has moved since the click. Barricade's facing (`_spawn_barricade`) and the animation aim (`_card_aim_point`) still read the live cursor. They belong to other slices and were left alone.

## Flagged

- **Push**: "Move a *unit* away from you". Should Push also target allies (and summons), or is "unit" just loose wording for an enemy? Today it is enemy-only.
- **Quick Arrow**: "A free ranged attack" / "a free 0-cost Quick Arrow". Should it also cost 0 tempo (it costs 2), or does "free" mean mana only?
- **Return Cut**: what does "counter with a melee strike" hit for? Today it is STR-only damage with no base (`get_effective_physical_damage(0)`, 1 at low STR). Should it be the auto attack's swing (base 3 through the same pipeline), or a number on the card?
- *(Not a wiring issue, noted for the art pass.)* Rise's structure is a procedural `CylinderMesh` (`Main._spawn_pillar`), which goes against CLAUDE.md's "Art: Packs First" rule. A pack's cliff or rock piece would replace it.
